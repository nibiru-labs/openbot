### Added

- A chat can stop the work of an agent and send your message in one step. While the agent works and
  your message waits in the composer, a "Stop and send" button appears above it. It stops the work
  and sends the message. When the stop fails, nothing is sent and your message stays in the
  composer.
- The messages of one sender read as one run. The time of a run of messages shows once, at its top,
  and the name of the sender shows once for the run, as on the desktop.
- A routine the agent created or changed shows as a card in the chat. It opens the routine, where
  its schedule is edited.
